# Звук двигателя / Engine recordings

Real petrol four-cylinder recordings, seamless-looped and normalised for this game:

- `engine_mid.wav` — "Engine sounds(2)" (`engine_sound.mp3`), https://opengameart.org/content/engine-sounds2 — **CC0 1.0**.
- `engine_low.wav`, `engine_hi1.wav`, `engine_hi2.wav` — from "racing car engine sound loops",
  https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops — **CC0 1.0**
  (cut from a public-domain car start-up recording, pdsounds.org).

Loops are high-passed at 25 Hz and cross-faded at their seams. They play at a rate that follows the
revs (EngineSamples.ts); the tappet, carburettor and fan layers are still synthesised.
