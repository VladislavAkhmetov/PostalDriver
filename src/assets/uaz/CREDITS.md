# UAZ-452 «буханка»

"UAZ-452 Utility Van Lowpoly" by **Mehozavr** — https://opengameart.org/content/uaz-452-utility-van-lowpoly
License: **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication).

482 faces, hand-painted diffuse texture. `uaz-452.glb` is converted from the
author's `uaz_452.fbx`; `UazPost.png` is the author's grey texture repainted
in a 1990s Russian post livery for this game. The original texture and wheel
texture are kept alongside.
